# Matrix integration review

The RuneBench-inspired redesign is a presentation of historical evidence. It
does not yet define a new four-model × four-class experiment. This review is
based on the September 14 dashboard/catalog diff and the existing score and
publication contracts; it does not change that other working checkout.

Current cells group exact model identities by protocol, class/task and frozen
fixture identity. They retain failed, unknown, unfinished, zero and negative
outcomes. The twelve historical model pilots cover four models on three classes.
The four recent scripted class checks have no model and cannot fill missing
model cells. There is no valid sixteen-run comparison yet.

Keep the layout, but make these distinctions visible before using it for the
new baseline:

1. Label the selected metric, horizon and evidence status beside the matrix.
   Historical five-minute pilots use mean saved net XP and remain unranked.
   A future native-window view must identify its different peak-rate metric.
2. Put “Scripted · no model · unranked” beside native videos. Do not describe
   an unverified skill such as Flash Jump as demonstrated by a clip.
3. Declare the fresh suite explicitly: four class fixtures, four exact agent
   configurations, policy/toolkit fingerprints, horizon and planned attempts.
   An empty declared cell is pending, not zero. Historical runs stay separate.
4. Preserve evidence checks when changing presentation, including older
   packages that do not contain optional illustration assets.

Toolkit qualification is the immediate gate. Then freeze the permanent ordinary
observe/act/replan baseline and collect its first complete pilot matrix, retaining
all outcomes. Repetitions and randomized order are needed for uncertainty and a
reliable comparison. Reflection or search conditions receive separate identities
and budgets; they do not replace the baseline.

The longer research framing is a separate stage: its 1800-second horizon,
authoritative fixed XP windows, normalization and suite aggregation need their
own qualification. Do not average raw XP across classes, mix horizons, replace
missing cells with zero or normalize against scripted controls to manufacture
an overall leaderboard.
