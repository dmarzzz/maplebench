# Task: maximize XP

You control a level-15 Warrior in a fresh deterministic MapleBench world.

Your goal is to gain as much experience as possible before the 10-minute episode ends.

You may inspect the world and write TypeScript programs using the MapleBench SDK. You may move, attack, use learned skills/items, loot drops, enter portals, and allocate earned AP/SP. You do not have privileged server mutation tools or autonomous helpers such as `grind()`.

The score is the sum of server-authoritative XP gain events during the episode.
