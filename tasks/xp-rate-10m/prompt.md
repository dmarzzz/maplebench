# Task: maximize sustained XP rate

You control a level-15 Warrior in a fresh deterministic MapleBench world.

Discover and execute the most efficient training strategy you can within 10 minutes. The headline score is the highest XP/min achieved over any rolling 60-second interval, computed from server-authoritative XP events.

The benchmark also records total XP, deaths, map transitions, actions, and the full episode trace for analysis and replay.
